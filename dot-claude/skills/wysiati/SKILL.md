# WYSIATI

What You See Is All There Is.

## Purpose

The mind fills gaps with the visible data and forgets the denominator, the
base rate, and the unseen population. A big percentage usually turns out to
be the base rate wearing a costume, or a denominator that quietly excluded
everyone who would have lowered it.

## Trigger

Any percentage or rate, especially a big one, and any claim using "most",
"almost all", or "nearly half".

## Procedure

1. **State the denominator out loud.** Who is in it, who is excluded, and
   what fraction of the real population the denominator represents.
2. **State the base rate.** What would this percentage be if nothing
   interesting were happening? If the claim is close to the base rate, there
   is no finding.
3. **Name the unseen population.** Who is NOT in this data? Untouched cases,
   unflagged cases, missing data, cases outside the window. Estimate their
   size. If they are large relative to the denominator, the percentage is
   about the visible subset, not the world.
4. **Reframe with the full population.** Recompute the rate against the
   widest defensible denominator and report both numbers.

## Output Contract

- Denominator: who is in, who is out, what share of the real population.
- Base rate and how the claim compares to it.
- Unseen population: who, estimated size.
- Both rates: visible-subset rate and full-population rate.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED / REFUTED / ABSTAINED. A rate reported without its
  denominator and base rate is REFUTED as a claim about the world.

## Operating Rules

- "44% of X" without X defined is not a sentence, it is a sound effect.
- The unseen population is not a footnote. If it is bigger than the
  denominator, it is the story.
- Pairs with doesnt-add-up: this skill covers steps 2 and 9 of that skill's
  checklist in depth.
